Three ways to arrive

Rough sketches of the first 30 seconds, drawn as real pixel art and animated. They're here to show you the feel, not the final art. Move your mouse over C.

A. The Night Walk

Side view. You're a small figure in a huge, quiet place, with one light on far ahead. Your projects and story are places you reach along the way.

Closest to Hollow Knight's mood: small you, big world, nothing explained.

B. The Room With an Extra Door

Top-down view. You start in your cozy 2 AM room, redrawn properly, and walk around it. It feels safe, except for a door that shouldn't be there.

Keeps the room you already have. Closest to Skyrim's "what's behind that?" pull.

C. Lights Out

Your cursor is a lantern in a dark room. Only the monitor glows by itself. Whatever you shine the light on, you find: notes on the wall, a map, a door.

The most mysterious, and instantly playable with no instructions.
