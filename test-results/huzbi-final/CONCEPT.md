# Huzbi, the host: concept choice

Drawn from the traits in `src/data/personal.ts` (Karachi CS grad, systems and AI, manga, tea at 2 AM,
cozy laziness), not from the old sprites. The player is a lanky hoodie kid with on-head headphones.

- **A Cardigan owl (chosen):** round head, big messy hair, round amber glasses, terracotta open cardigan
  over a cream tee, headphones hung round the neck, tea mug, house slippers. Wide and soft: the silhouette
  is a circle on a rounded trapezoid, the opposite of the player's tall narrow hoodie. The glasses give the
  face something to do in every frame (pushed up under the manga, peeking over a page, glinting when awake).
- **B Blanket heron:** tall, top-bun, rectangular glasses, wrapped in a checked throw. Too close to the
  player's tall-and-narrow silhouette, and the blanket hides the arms we need for acting.
- **C Kotatsu loaf:** a head on a heap of blanket. Funny, but nothing to animate below the neck.

A wins: distinct silhouette, amber and terracotta read against the teal sofa and are not the player's blue,
and the arms, glasses, mug and manga all stay visible for the wake-up and the reading loop.
